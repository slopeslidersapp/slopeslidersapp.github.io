Privacy Policy

Last updated: 7 August 2026

Slope Sliders is a single-player game. It has no accounts, no login, and no server of its own. This policy explains the little data involved and who handles it.

What the game stores

Your progress is saved only on your device, using local browser-style storage inside the app. That includes:

  • Which routes you have completed and your star ratings
  • Your best times and best Free Ski distance
  • Which mountains you have unlocked
  • Sound and music preferences

None of this is transmitted anywhere. It is not backed up to us, and we cannot see it. Deleting the app deletes it. You can also erase it at any time from Settings → Reset Progress inside the game.

What we collect

Nothing. We do not operate a server, we do not have analytics, and we do not ask for your name, email, location or contacts.

Advertising

The game offers an optional rewarded video ad: if you crash, you may choose to watch an ad in exchange for one extra life. It is entirely optional and nothing in the game is gated behind it.

Those ads are served by Google AdMob. To serve and measure ads, Google may collect and process information such as your device's advertising identifier, device type and operating system, coarse location derived from your IP address, and how you interacted with an ad. That data is handled by Google under its own policies, not ours:

  • Google Privacy Policy
  • How Google uses data from AdMob partners

Tracking and your choice

On iOS you will be asked, through Apple's App Tracking Transparency prompt, whether to allow tracking. If you decline, the game works exactly the same. Ads still appear when you choose to watch one; they are simply less personalised. You can change your answer any time in Settings → Privacy & Security → Tracking, and you can reset or disable your advertising identifier in Settings → Privacy & Security → Apple Advertising.

Children

Slope Sliders is not directed at children under 13 and is not submitted under the App Store Kids Category. We do not knowingly collect personal information from children. If you believe a child has provided personal information, contact us and we will address it.

Changes

If this policy changes, the revised version will be posted on this page with an updated date.
