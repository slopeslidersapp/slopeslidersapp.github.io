Slope Sliders

Pixel-art downhill skiing. One thumb.

Tap to change direction and carve your way down the mountain. Hold a line and you pick up speed; tap and you scrub it off. That's the whole control scheme, and it takes about four seconds to learn.

What's in it

  • Seven mountains, 80+ routes - each one unlocks the next
  • Three-star times on every route, so there's always a faster line
  • Wildlife that reacts - deer bolt, birds scatter, and bears give chase
  • Avalanches, cliffs, deep powder and ice
  • Endless Free Ski for when you just want to see how far you get
